# FineGuard

#### A comprehensive fine management system designed to eliminate long queues and streamline the process for schools and colleges.

---

## Overview

FineGuard was born out of a personal frustration. After spending 9 hours in a queue just to pay a fine, I realized that the current fine management systems are severely broken. I built FineGuard to solve this problem for everyone.

The platform provides a seamless experience for both teachers and students, ensuring transparency and efficiency in handling fines.

---

## Tech Stack

- **Next.js** (Frontend & API Routes)
- **Integrated Backend**
- **TypeScript**
- **MongoDB**
- **Tailwind CSS**

---

## Why I Built This

I faced a problem where I had to spend 9 hours to pay a fine because the institution didn't have a functional fine management system. I decided to build a solution that makes this process digital, fast, and transparent.

Solving this problem while building [fine.tanishtirpathi.me](https://fine.tanishtirpathi.me) was a great learning experience in full-stack development.

---

## Core Features

- **Teacher Portal**: Teachers can easily add students and issue fines.
- **Student Portal**: Students can view their fines and pay them online instantly.
- **Real-time Tracking**: Complete transparency for both parties.
